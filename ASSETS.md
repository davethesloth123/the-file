# Asset provenance

The visual assets added or regenerated for this gameplay pass are project-owned procedural outputs:

- Character GLBs in `public/models/` are generated by `tools/character-generator.py` from the authored specifications in `src/data/archetypes.json`.
- Surface and foliage textures in `public/textures/` are generated by `tools/texture-generator.py` from project-authored procedural patterns.
- World geometry, vehicles, stairs, props, foliage, signs, and character accessories are assembled by project source code.

No paid or third-party art assets were introduced in this pass, so there are no additional external art licenses or attribution requirements.
